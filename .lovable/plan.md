# ANEL 007 Grocery Store Website

## Overview
Build a polished one-page storefront for ANEL 007 that feels warm, hospitable, and premium. The page will use an airy cream-and-beige palette, warm food-inspired accents, rounded blocks, restrained shadows, and high-quality product photography.

## Page structure
- Add a compact header with the ANEL 007 wordmark, category links, and a prominent “Order Now” action.
- Create a first-screen hero centered on the ANEL 007 name, a short premium-quality slogan, a clear call to action, and an appetizing sausage image as the main visual focus.
- Add a scannable assortment section for cured meats, sausages, fresh produce, and cheeses.
- Include a curated-products area with strong imagery, product names, and concise descriptions without inventing prices.
- Add a short hospitality/quality story section and a simple footer with navigation only, avoiding invented contact details or opening hours.

## Visual direction
- Light cream and beige surfaces with burgundy, tomato, herb-green, and golden accents.
- Soft block-based composition with generous spacing, smooth corners, subtle depth, and no generic gradient-heavy styling.
- Refined display typography paired with a readable modern sans serif.
- Generate cohesive, appetizing food photography for the sausage hero and supporting product categories; keep imagery bright, tactile, and editorial rather than stock-like.
- Add subtle entrance and image-hover motion, while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Ensure the layout remains polished and readable on mobile and desktop.

## Interactions
- Header links scroll to the relevant page sections.
- “Order Now” and “View Menu” actions lead visitors directly to the assortment/products section.
- Product blocks have clear interactive feedback without adding checkout or ordering logic.

## Technical details
- Replace the placeholder home page at `/` and keep the experience frontend-only.
- Extend the existing semantic design tokens for the chosen palette and typography.
- Add page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Use locally bundled generated imagery and accessible text alternatives.
- Validate the live page for build errors, visual balance, overflow, and mobile responsiveness.
